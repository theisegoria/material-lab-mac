# Material Lab for Mac

Turn a photo of a surface into a complete **PBR material**: albedo, height, normal, roughness, ambient occlusion and
more, on your Mac with no account and nothing uploaded.

- **Photo to material in seconds:** delighting, perspective correction, albedo repair and make-tileable with seam
  quality scores.
- **Learned geometry:** the bundled MoGe-2 model recovers surface form from a single photo, fused with fine detail
  from the image.
- **39 material presets** (brick, stone, wood, metal, fabric and more), recipes you can save, and authored inputs for
  hand-painted albedo or your own maps.
- **Export anywhere:** glTF, USDZ, MaterialX, Unreal, Unity, Godot, Blender, Substance, Roblox and Minecraft LabPBR,
  plus a batch queue for whole folders.
- **Live 3D preview** with lighting environments, tiling and side-by-side compare.

## Download
Get **MaterialLab-1.3.1-macOS.dmg** from [Releases](https://github.com/theisegoria/material-lab-mac/releases/latest)
and drag Material Lab to Applications.

Requires macOS 27 on Apple silicon. The app is signed with a Developer ID but not notarized. The first time, right-click it in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again.

The free command-line tool is at [theisegoria/material-lab-cli](https://github.com/theisegoria/material-lab-cli).
