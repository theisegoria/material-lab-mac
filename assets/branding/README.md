# Material Lab icon sources

The two text-free, 1024 × 1024 source images were generated as a pair with the bundled imagegen CLI using gpt-image-1.5, quality=high, size=1024x1024, and batch concurrency 2. The first attempt left the marks unreadable at 16–32 pixels. The approved second pair enlarges the marks; both were visually inspected at 16, 32, 60, 128, and 256 pixels. Native app icon resources are derived from these approved source images.

## Material Lab for Mac

Prompt: Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: full-bleed native macOS app icon artwork for a general-purpose PBR Material Lab. Create a bold, simple abstract mark of a copper material slab transforming into topographic layers and one large cyan-to-violet normal facet. The slab and facet TOGETHER fill at least 78 percent of the canvas width and 75 percent of the height; crop close, no distant floating object, no empty border or generous padding. Full square graphite background extends to every edge so the OS can mask corners. Clear silhouette and high contrast at 16 pixels, thick contours only, no tiny detail. Crisp dimensional vector-like design. No text, letters, numerals, logos, trademarks, device mockup, frame, watermark, or white margins. Coordinated with companion iPhone icon.

## Material Capture for iPhone

Prompt: Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: full-bleed native iOS app icon artwork for a general-purpose PBR Material Capture companion. Create a bold, simple cyan-and-violet camera corner frame around a copper surface square with a large blue-violet normal facet and two broad topographic contours. The frame and copper surface TOGETHER fill at least 82 percent of the canvas width and height; crop close, no distant floating object, no empty border or generous padding. Full square graphite background extends to every edge so iOS can mask corners. Readable high-contrast silhouette at 16 pixels, thick strokes, no tiny fine detail. Crisp dimensional vector-like design, visual sibling of a copper material slab desktop icon. No text, letters, numerals, logos, trademarks, device mockup, watermark, or white margins.
