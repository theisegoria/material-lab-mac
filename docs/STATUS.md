# Material Lab implementation status — 2026-09-24 (algorithm 1.4.0)

Material Lab is a standalone, general-purpose local tool. Its C++23 derivation core is compiled into the macOS 27 and iOS 27 apps and exposed through the Mac CLI. Materials remain estimates, and no bundle is automatically admitted into a game runtime.

## Implemented

- **Core 1.3.0.** +Y / green-up normals with a DirectX option; GTAO cosine-weighted horizon AO with per-pixel open-sky normalization and an analytic bent normal; Scharr gradients; log-luminance relief bands scaled to a per-preset rms-slope prior; all spatial scales at a 1024 reference; range-normalized 16-bit height with its depth span in the manifest; learned form fused with albedo detail by a frequency split, with the learned field orienting the detail; cue-driven roughness within a preset range; delighting; make-tileable with an aligned minimum-error seam cut (default) or a histogram-preserving blend; periodic multigrid integration when tiling; deterministic row-parallel derivation.
- **Learned geometry.** MoGe-2 ViT-S normals (MIT) converted to a 518² fp16 Core ML model. The tiling contract (wrap padding and a periodic fold when tiling, two orientations per tile, per-tile affine detilt, working size up to 2072) is implemented once in the core (`PBRLabGuideTiler`). CLI (`--learned-normals`), macOS (Settings › Learned Geometry, built-in model, Learned geometry slider) and iOS. Every export records the model identity, compute units, tiling and a hash of the guide normals.
- **CLI.** Hardened JSON handling (orderly exit 1, no aborts), symlink- and traversal-safe catalogs, a validator that rejects forged manifests, atomic in-memory PNG writing with adaptive filtering, concurrent map encoding, EXIF orientation, unpremultiplied decode, a timed-out and signal-forwarded pre-upscale child, and order-independent recipe flags.
- **macOS app.** Native Liquid Glass design: sidebar, canvas with floating glass controls, inspector with grouped recipe sections, map strip with ⌘1–9, before/after split, 3×3 tile view with a tiling-quality card, RealityKit PBR preview, debounced live re-derivation, per-source recipes, drag and drop, Bonjour/TLS pairing with per-capture approval. Core compiled at -O3.
- **iOS app.** Full-bleed camera with glass controls, level and glare hints, Vision auto-crop, detented recipe sheet, live 512² preview, 512–4K output, tiled / lit sphere / compare modes, capture persistence, always-on privacy scrubbing of originals, Files export and pinned local transfer.

## Remaining proof limits

- **LiDAR depth guidance (2026-10-07).** On LiDAR iPhones the camera uses the LiDAR depth camera, and a Depth toggle embeds the depth map in the photo (no Constant Color or RAW in the same shot). Depth found in any imported photo (`DepthCapture.read`) can drive a recipe's "LiDAR depth" option. `DepthGuide` maps the corrected crop through the quad homography and EXIF orientation into the depth map, back-projects it with the calibration intrinsics, fits the surface plane, and expresses smoothed normals in the plane's tangent frame as guide normals, blended evenly with MoGe-2 when both are on (provenance `lidar-depth`). Verified on synthetic depth: a wall tilted 35° comes out flat; a ridge's flanks have the right signs in landscape and portrait (EXIF 6) sensor frames; a JPEG with embedded DepthFloat32 is detected, guides derivation (normal green 131 above / 122 below an 8 mm ridge), and its bundle validates with the CLI. Real LiDAR noise, the depth-camera capture path and its interaction with Constant Color are untested without a Pro iPhone.
- **Flash/no-flash albedo (2026-10-07).** On iPhones without Constant Color, Flash pair takes an ambient and a flash photo, aligns them (Vision homographic registration), subtracts the ambient light scaled by the EXIF exposure ratio, and divides out the flash fall-off fitted as a log-quadratic, giving the albedo photo; failures fall back to the ambient photo with a note. Verified on synthetic scenes: lighting spread (std of log image/albedo) falls below 35 % of the ambient photo's under a strong gradient and cast shadow; registration undoes a 6 × 4 px shift. Untested on a device.
- Device-only and untested here: ProRAW development (`CameraController.developProRAW`), `BGContinuedProcessingTask` for 2K/4K, AR placement in Quick Look, the capture coach, and the LiDAR and flash-pair capture paths above. Both device builds (simulator and iphoneos) compile.
- Real Liquid Glass captures of the Mac app (B7) need Screen Recording permission or computer use; the harness renders glass as flat regions.
- Simulator-only validation on iOS. No physical camera, Constant Color hardware, or real-device local-network permission behaviour has been tested.
- Whole-window screen capture is unavailable on this machine, so the macOS Liquid Glass surfaces were verified through the app's own off-screen renders, which draw glass and vibrancy as flat regions. The layout, controls and map rendering were checked; the material of the glass itself was not.
- The Neural Engine runs the learned model in fp16; output can differ slightly between chips and OS versions. CPU-only mode is more reproducible.
- Mac/iOS pixel parity has not been re-measured for algorithms 1.2.0 or 1.3.0. The 1.3.0 app changes are build-verified only.
- A 1K learned derivation now takes about 3 s (50 model passes) and 4K about 19 s (162 passes).
- Relief amplitude is a prior (per-preset target slope × `height_strength`); an albedo cannot tell a polished slab from a rough one of the same pattern.
- Algorithm 1.0.0 bundles have inverted green in their normal maps; the validator warns, and they should be re-derived.
- The release app is Developer ID signed but not notarized.
